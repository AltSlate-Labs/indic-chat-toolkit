Indic chat → English

Type a message the way people text: Hinglish ("yaar kal ki meeting cancel ho gayi"), or Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi or Urdu, romanized or in native script. You get English.

Model: altslate/indic-chat-mt-small (483M, MIT), run in your browser with int8 weights and greedy decoding, which are a little less accurate than the full model with beam search. Your text never leaves your device. The first run downloads about 730 MB; "Auto-detect" adds about 140 MB for altslate/indic-chat-lid-small. Hinglish works best. In other languages, about one message in two loses some meaning, and negation is sometimes lost in Tamil and Telugu; see the model card.
