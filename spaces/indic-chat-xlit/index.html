Indic chat transliteration

Type a message the way people text in the Latin alphabet ("kal meeting cancel ho gayi") and get it in native script, for Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu, Sindhi and Sinhala.

Model: altslate/indic-chat-xlit-small (300M, Apache-2.0), run in your browser with int8 weights, which are slightly less accurate than the full model. Your text never leaves your device. The first run downloads about 300 MB; "Auto-detect" adds about 180 MB for altslate/indic-chat-lid-small. By default English words are written in native script too; tick "Keep English words" to leave them in Latin script. Ambiguous spellings are sometimes wrong; see the model card.
