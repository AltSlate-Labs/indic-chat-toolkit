Screenshot PII redactor

Drop a screenshot of a chat, an app or a document, and the personal data in it is covered: Aadhaar, PAN, UPI IDs, phone numbers, bank accounts, IFSC, GSTIN, emails, passport, driving licence, voter ID, vehicle numbers, PIN codes, names, addresses and dates of birth, in English, Hindi and Hinglish (Latin or Devanagari script).

Everything runs in your browser; the image never leaves your device. Text is read with PaddlePaddle's PP-OCRv5 models (Apache-2.0; a Latin recognizer and a Devanagari one, the more confident reading wins per line) and the personal data is found with altslate/indic-pii-redactor-small. The first run downloads about 170 MB. On rendered test screenshots it covered 96% of Latin-script and 90% of Devanagari personal data; it misses some names and dates and can't read other scripts yet. Always check the result before sharing.
