Indic TTS normalizer

Paste text with numbers and get the words a text-to-speech voice should say: ₹ amounts, times, dates, years, percentages, decimals, phone numbers, units, ordinals, ranges and cricket scores, in Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi and Urdu.

Model: altslate/indic-tts-normalizer-small (300M, Apache-2.0), run in your browser with int8 weights. Your text never leaves your device. The first run downloads about 300 MB; after that a sentence takes a few seconds (the output is written byte by byte). It reads one pattern per format (for example 12/03/2024 is always day/month/year) and was not checked by native speakers; see the model card for results and limitations.
