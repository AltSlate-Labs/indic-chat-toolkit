Indic chat language ID

Tells you which language a short message is in, for Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu, Sindhi and Sinhala, whether typed in native script or in the Latin alphabet ("kal mere interview tha"). It also has en and other.

Model: altslate/indic-chat-lid-small (140M, Apache-2.0), run in your browser. Your text never leaves your device. The first run downloads about 180 MB (model and tokenizer), which is then cached. Romanized Hindi and Urdu are often the same text, and other Indic languages (Assamese, Konkani, Nepali…) may be labelled as a neighbour; see the model card.
