Demo of [altslate/indic-chat-lid-small](https://huggingface.co/altslate/indic-chat-lid-small), a 140M
language-identification model for short chat messages in Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati,
Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu, Sindhi and Sinhala, typed in native script or in the Latin alphabet.
It runs entirely in your browser with [transformers.js](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers.js) and the
model's int8 ONNX file and tokenizer (about 180 MB, downloaded once and cached). Your text never leaves your device.
See the model card for results and limitations.
